# Image credits and provenance

Every product image on this site is a stock photograph from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com),
used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits commercial use
without attribution. Credit is recorded here anyway, so the source of each image is traceable.

## These are not photographs of our products

None of these images shows a Sarvha bottle. They show the plant, fruit, seed or wood the oil
is distilled from. That is a deliberate choice: a stock photo of someone else's amber bottle
presented as our product would be a misrepresentation, and customers would notice on delivery.
The alt text describes what is actually in each photograph and never claims it is the product.

Replace these with real photographs of stock as soon as there is stock to photograph. Note that
Google Merchant Center requires product images to show the actual product and rejects stock or
illustrative images for most categories, so these will not serve for Shopping listings.
