Ritual guide

Three simple ways to use

◌

Diffuse

Add 4–6 drops to water in an ultrasonic diffuser to scent a room in minutes.

✋

Apply

Dilute 2–3 drops in a teaspoon of carrier oil like Jojoba before applying to skin.

♨

Bath & steam

Mix 5 drops with bath salts, or add to a bowl of hot water and breathe deeply.

Always dilute before topical use and patch-test first. Not for internal use. Consult a doctor if pregnant, nursing or under medical care.

Are your oils 100% pure?

Yes. Every batch is GC/MS tested by an independent lab, and we never add carriers, synthetic fragrance or fillers to our essential oils.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 24 hours on business days and usually arrive in 3–5 days. Shipping is free over .

Can I apply oils directly on my skin?

We recommend always diluting with a carrier oil — roughly 2–3 drops per teaspoon — and doing a patch test first.

What's your return policy?

If you're not happy, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

How should I store my oils?

Keep bottles tightly capped, away from sunlight and heat. Most oils stay fresh for 2–3 years; citrus oils for about 1 year.
